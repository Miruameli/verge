### Why this entry was written late

`v0.2.0` shipped with a full set of artifacts but no entry of its own, while
`v0.1.0` and `v0.3.0` both had one. Found during the crates.io backfill audit on
2026-10-05, where three tags were being treated alike and only two of them had a
release record. Closed by this entry rather than left as an unexplained gap.

The artifact facts are read from GitHub, not reconstructed from memory. The one
item that cannot be recovered retroactively is the checksum re-verification
above, and it is marked as such rather than assumed to have passed.
